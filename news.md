## Lead — APEC, Shenzhen
- Xi Jinping greeted Donald Trump on the tarmac at Shenzhen airport on
  Wednesday 18 Nov; Air Force One landed ~16:45, greeting ~17:05 Shenzhen time.
  First time in ~50 years a Chinese leader has greeted a visiting dignitary
  at the airport; a hug, a red carpet, a J-10C flypast.
- Trump, Xi and Putin shook hands ahead of the APEC family photo; the
  first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral is set for Thursday 19 Nov, to discuss
  nuclear disarmament, Ukraine, Iran, trade and AI. Putin arrived early
  Wednesday and was met by Cai Qi.
- Sources: Nikkei Asia live blog (18 Nov, 21:08 JST update);
  Anadolu Agency (18 Nov); AFP via Korea Times (published 18 Nov 16:28 UTC).

## Gulf — US / Iran strikes widen
- US fighter jets struck an IRGC missile launch site on Qeshm Island in the
  Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday evening (self-defence strike, precision
  munitions), after Iranian drones targeted a US radar at Bahrain's
  Sheikh Isa Air Base.
- On Wednesday 18 Nov Iran's army said its drones targeted Patriot systems
  and drone/missile launch platforms at Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait;
  Kuwait said its air defences were countering hostile drones; Bahrain said
  it intercepted further Iranian attacks (fourth in six days).
- Sources: Reuters via GMA Network (18 Nov 18:11); Channel News Asia
  (published 18 Nov 11:19 +08:00); Gulf News live (18 Nov).

## Cyclone Akvan — Bay of Bengal
- Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan, ~580–610 km off the Andhra coast on the
  morning of 18 Nov, moving slowly north-northwest at ~3 km/h.
- IMD forecast: landfall between Nellore and Visakhapatnam (later updated
  Machilipatnam–Puri) around noon Friday 20 Nov as a severe cyclonic storm,
  winds 100–110 km/h gusting 120 km/h; third cyclone to threaten Andhra
  Pradesh in a month.
- Sources: The Hindu live updates (18 Nov 22:04 IST); IMD via Indian
  Express live tracker (18 Nov); Mathrubhumi (18 Nov 11:11 IST).

## Gaza — new Israeli operation
- Israeli military announced a "concentrated operation" in three areas of
  central Gaza (blocks 660, 661, 666), west of the "Yellow Line", on
  Wednesday 18 Nov — the first broad ground offensive publicly announced
  since the October 2025 ceasefire. Tanks and bulldozers advanced toward
  Salah al-Din Road; families fled Nuseirat/Bureij areas; three
  Palestinians reported killed on Wednesday.
- Sources: Al Jazeera (published 18 Nov 14:39 UTC); Outlook India live
  blog (18 Nov 17:59 IST); TRT World (18 Nov).

## Manila — Quad meets; trade deal claimed
- Trump met Quad leaders (Modi, Japan's Takaichi, Australia's Albanese) in
  Manila on the sidelines of the 49th ASEAN Summit, then held bilateral
  talks with President Marcos Jr. at Malacanang.
- Trump announced a "very big trade deal, the biggest trade deal we've ever
  made by far"; Philippine officials said nothing was signed — only an
  understanding on economic cooperation, no text, no terms released.
- Sources: GMA Network (18 Nov 14:53); Philstar (18 Nov, updated 16:17);
  Reuters via WUTV29 (18 Nov).
